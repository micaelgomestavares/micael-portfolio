# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, potential clients, and collaborators evaluating Micael Gomes' technical range, product judgment, and ability to deliver polished web experiences. They should understand his profile and strongest work quickly, then have clear paths to inspect projects, download a CV, or make contact.

## Product Purpose

Present Micael as a full-stack software developer who combines strong frontend craft with dependable backend experience. Success means earning qualified conversations for both employment and freelance work without making either audience feel secondary.

## Brand Personality

Precise, energetic, and assured. The experience should feel like a well-calibrated technical instrument: disciplined enough for engineering teams, expressive enough to demonstrate frontend taste, and direct in its language.

## Anti-references

Generic AI portfolio templates; purple-blue aurora gradients; identical floating cards; excessive glassmorphism; decorative badges without hierarchy; every section using the same fade-up reveal; effects that obscure content; visual language that feels like a gaming landing page; dense interfaces that make recruiters hunt for the important facts.

## Design Principles

1. Show proof before claims: projects and concrete experience carry the story.
2. Motion demonstrates craft: animation must clarify hierarchy, progression, or interaction.
3. Technical, not theatrical: unusual details stay precise and purposeful.
4. Two audiences, one path: hiring and freelance contact actions remain equally visible.
5. Fast at first glance, rewarding on inspection: the essentials scan quickly while deeper details invite exploration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Preserve visible focus states, semantic landmarks, and clear link labels. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with a composed static experience, and never make animation required to access content.
